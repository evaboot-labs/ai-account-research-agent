# Who to find at each account

Example: replace it with your own buyers, or let your agent ask you.

- **Roles:** the sales leaders who own pipeline. Chief Revenue Officer, Chief Sales Officer, SVP or VP of Sales, Head of Sales, Sales Director.
- **Not:** assistants, coordinators, analysts, interns, pre-sales, sales engineering, sales enablement.
- **Per company:** the 3 most senior.
- **Small companies with no sales title:** the founder or CEO.

If you change the roles, ask your agent to update `TITLES` and `RANKS` in `research.py` to match.
